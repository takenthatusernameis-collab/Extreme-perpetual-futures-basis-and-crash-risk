# Extreme Perpetual-Futures Basis and Crash Risk

**Research status:** initialized; hypothesis untested; no trading edge or predictive effect established.

This repository tests whether extreme perpetual-futures basis conditions forecast a higher frequency and severity of underlying-asset crashes over the next 7–28 calendar days. The primary hypothesis is directional: unusually rich positive perpetual basis may signal crowded-long exposure. Cheap negative basis and absolute basis extremes are separate secondary hypotheses. Average returns are not the primary target.

The research loop uses free public historical data and open-source tooling only. It must expand candidate discovery in descending market-cap rank without a fixed top-N cutoff, while using only defensible point-in-time market-cap ranks for historical universe membership. Failed, null, unfavorable, incomplete, and blocked results remain recorded.

No paid services, credentials, live trading, paper trading, or orders.
